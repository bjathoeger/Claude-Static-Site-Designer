# QualiWare blog: working decisions

ID: abigail1

Author: Abigail

Scope: a new QualiWare blog at qualiware.com/blog (a page path on the main site, not a subdomain), covering the blog's overview page, the subpage for each post, and the filters. The footer is not covered: the blog reuses the main site's footer.

Status: draft (22 Oct 2026). These decisions guide the design of the new blog.

## Why the blog is being made
The website often doesn't give enough examples of how QualiWare's product works. The blog should fill that gap with stories that show the product at work, and bring traffic and leads to the site.

## Audience
- **Main:** enterprise architects, some already customers and some not. Usually a little more technically minded.
- **Secondary:** GRC (governance, risk and compliance) managers, who are less technically minded.

## Core message
QualiWare stays up to date with the challenges enterprise architects face.

## What the blog must do
1. **Drive traffic** to the site through storytelling, for both people and LLMs.
2. **Generate leads**, by sending readers on to pages with a lead form, such as product pages and recorded webinars.
3. **Explain how QualiWare's product works**, with concrete examples.
4. **Look visibly different** from the rest of qualiware.com, so the blog stands out as its own place.

## How we'll know it works
Not set yet.

## Blog overview page (decided)
| Part | Covers |
|---|---|
| Post cards | Each post shows a large image with only a little text, its categories and its publication date. |
| "Read more" | Each card links to the post's own subpage. |
| Filters | Sort and filter posts alphabetically, chronologically and by category. No other filters. |
| Search | Search across posts. |

## Post subpage (decided)
Header → subheader → body text → footer (the main site's footer). Whether the page opens with a large image is not decided yet (see Open questions).
- Images can be inserted in the body text.
- Each post has categories and a publication date.
- Posts can link to product pages or to external news pages.

## Categories (decided)
The blog keeps the current blog's categories: News · User Tips · Analyst Recognition · QW Inside · QualiWare CEO Blog Post · France.

## Existing posts (decided)
All posts from the current blog (qualiware.com/blogs) move to the new blog.

## Calls to action
- **Main:** "Read more" from the overview page to each post. From a post, links to pages with a lead form, such as product pages and recorded webinars. Posts can also link to external news pages.
- **Smaller first step:** Not set yet.

## Proof available
| Proof | Backs |
|---|---|
| Customer stories (the "Customer Stories" section on qualiware.com) | Posts that show how the product works in practice |
| "QualiWare Recognized as a Champion" in the SoftwareReviews Emotional Footprint Diamond (current blog post) | Analyst Recognition posts |
| Gartner's first Magic Quadrant for Digital Twin of an Organization Platforms (current blog post; QualiWare's placement is not stated in the text) | Analyst Recognition and digital twin posts |

Gaps: which customer stories can be used is not set yet. No stats about QualiWare were found on the current blog.

## Translations
- **Default:** English
- **Other languages:** Swedish · French · Spanish · German

Every post is translated into all four other languages.

## Left out on purpose
- The footer: the blog uses the main site's footer, so this brief doesn't design one.

## Open questions
- **Targets.** What should the blog achieve, for example in traffic, leads or demo requests?
- **Visual style.** How different should the blog look from qualiware.com? Should it keep anything, such as the logo or colors?
- **Smaller first step.** Is there a lighter call to action, for example a newsletter or webinar?
- **Proof.** Which customer stories, analyst reports and stats can posts use? Can the Gartner placement be named?
- **Top of the post subpage.** Should a post open with a large image, should it be up to each post, or should there be no image at the top?
